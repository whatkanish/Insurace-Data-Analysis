# Insurace-Data-Analysis
Insurance Data Analysis
📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an insurance dataset containing 15,000+ customer records to uncover meaningful insights about customer demographics, insurance policies, premiums, claims, and other factors affecting insurance business performance.

The goal is to use data analysis and visualization techniques to identify patterns, trends, customer segments, and factors that may influence insurance costs and claims.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer demographics and insurance characteristics.
Understand the distribution of insurance premiums.
Identify patterns in insurance claims.
Analyze the relationship between customer attributes and insurance costs.
Identify high-risk customer segments.
Explore factors that influence claim frequency and claim amounts.
Create meaningful visualizations and business insights.
Support data-driven decision-making for insurance companies.
📁 Dataset

The dataset contains more than 15,000 insurance records.

Typical features may include:

Feature	Description
Customer ID	Unique identifier for each customer
Age	Customer's age
Gender	Customer gender
Region	Customer's geographical region
Policy Type	Type of insurance policy
Premium	Insurance premium paid
Claim Amount	Amount claimed by the customer
Claim Status	Whether a claim was made
Policy Duration	Duration of the insurance policy
Vehicle/Asset Type	Type of insured asset
Risk Factors	Factors associated with customer risk
